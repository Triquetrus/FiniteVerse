Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FiniteVerse ("we", "the app") is a finite automata visualizer. This page explains what information we collect when you use it and how it's handled.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following from your Google account, via Firebase Authentication:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • A unique account identifier (UID)

This information is stored in our Firestore database, linked to your account, along with the timestamp of your last sign-in.

Why we collect it

Solely to authenticate you within the app. We do not currently offer a feature to save your automata between sessions — work you build exists only in your browser for that session.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for ads or targeted marketing.
  • We do not access your Google account beyond basic profile info (name, email, photo) needed for sign-in.

Data storage

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore), secured so that only your own signed-in account can read or write your own data.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
